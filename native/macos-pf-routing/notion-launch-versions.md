with Electron **43.6.0**. Other versions and version suffixes refuse. The existing
Apple anchor, `notion.id` identifier, vendor team `LBQJ96FQ8D`, strict nested-code
and all-architecture signature checks remain mandatory. Registered snapshots
retain their exact original content digest, executable identity and signature.

Electron remains 43.6.0. All four bundled Notion Helper applications carry version
7.37.1 and team `LBQJ96FQ8D`. Deep, strict, all-architecture vendor signature
verification passed; the main CDHash was
`37aa8e88609deb4bc1a8aa34c71e0b1bd682c95f`. This records the inspected copy, rather
than permitting arbitrary binaries with that version label.

The fixed SOCKS, resolver, user-data and locale arguments and the process-local
Seatbelt profile are unchanged. Matching launch metadata permits read-only
preparation; it does not prove live flag behavior, helper or external delegation
coverage, protected execution, or failure cleanup. Those acceptance gates remain
closed until the real application is tested. The inspection and inert fixtures
do not launch, copy or alter Notion, authorize a helper, or modify PF or host DNS.
